## Africa’s Hidden AI Bottleneck

### Sources

- [AI Made in Africa: Voices of AI Businesses Across Africa](https://www.giz.de/sites/default/files/media/els-document/2026-10/ai-made-africa-voices-ai-businesses-across-africa.pdf) · GIZ & Briter, October 2026. Survey of 85 African AI businesses, answers collected April to July 2026; 88% sell to other businesses. Barriers to scaling, with more than one answer allowed: customer trust and AI literacy 65%, high development and consumer costs 49%, integration with legacy systems 34%, long sales cycles 33%, data availability 33%, regulatory uncertainty 24%, talent availability 16%, currency volatility 7%.
- [Satya Nadella on private evaluations as core IP](https://www.thetwentyminutevc.com/satya-nadella) · 20VC, 26 October 2026. Interview with Harry Stebbings: every firm will have a private evaluation benchmark, and it will be its core IP.
- [Alexa Shopping: One Request, One Offer, No Screen](https://njenga.ai/alexa-shopping/) · Njenga Kariuki, 2017–2018. Firsthand record: 1,000 sampled voice shopping requests judged each week; a calibrated team of ten; the share ending in the right offer rose from about 50% in 2017 to 85% in 2018.
- [GDPval](https://openai.com/index/gdpval/) · OpenAI, September 2025. 1,320 real work tasks across 44 occupations in the nine sectors that contribute most to US GDP, written and graded by practitioners with an average of 14 years of experience.
- [AfroBench: How Good Are Large Language Models on African Languages?](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.976) · ACL Findings, 2025. Evaluates large language models across 64 African languages.
- [AfriMed-QA: A Pan-African, Multi-Specialty Medical QA Benchmark](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.96) · ACL, 2025. Medical question-answering benchmark built across 16 African countries.
- [African Languages Trust and Safety AI Benchmark](https://www.gsma.com/newsroom/blog/african-trust-safety-llm-benchmark-stress-testing-ai-safety-across-africas-languages-and-contexts/) · GSMA & Zindi, July 2026. 4,216 safety tests across eight African languages.
- [Statement on access to Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access) · Anthropic, June 2026. Access was suspended on 12 June 2026 to comply with US export controls and restored on 1 July 2026, 19 days later.
- [The Price of Progress: Price Performance and the Future of AI (arXiv:2511.23455v2)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23455) · MIT FutureTech, March 2026. Preprint estimate: the price of a given level of benchmark performance falls 5 to 10 times a year.
- [State of the Industry Report on Mobile Money 2026](https://www.gsma.com/sotir/) · GSMA, March 2026. Africa accounted for 66% of the world’s US$2.1 trillion in mobile money transaction value in 2025.
- [Four announcements of units that place engineers inside customers](https://openai.com/index/openai-launches-the-deployment-company) · OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS, Microsoft, May to July 2026. OpenAI Deployment Company, more than US$4 billion of initial investment (11 May 2026); Anthropic’s services firm with Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman and Goldman Sachs, about US$1.5 billion in press reports (4 May 2026); AWS Forward Deployed Engineering, US$1 billion (30 June 2026); Microsoft Frontier Company, US$2.5 billion (2 July 2026). The total of about US$9 billion is the sum of the four. The amounts were announced, not spent, and two include outside investors’ money.
- [What four charts reveal about the global jobs challenge](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/opendata/what-four-charts-reveal-about-the-global-jobs-challenge) · World Bank, 29 July 2026. Projection: Sub-Saharan Africa accounts for more than three-quarters of the growth in the world’s working-age population between 2025 and 2050.
